**Comments** will give you comment threads on anything in your app: a chat message, a file, an image, a page, or a few words in a shared document.

- A thread belongs to a **target**, a key you choose, such as `message:m_481` or `file:f_77`. An optional **anchor** says where in that target.
- Threads, replies, mentions and reactions.
- Resolve a thread, and reopen it.
- Only the author can edit a comment.
- A `'comment'` role, for people who can comment but not edit.
- Notifications through the [Notifications Feed](/plugins/notifications-feed/overview) plugin.
- With [Collaboration](/plugins/collaboration/overview), a thread can sit on some words or on one item of a shared document. It stays on them as people type and move things, and it shows when its words are deleted.

Comments works without Collaboration. An app that uses Collaboration never needs Comments, and an app that comments on chat messages never needs Collaboration.
