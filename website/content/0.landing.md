  #headline
  The Contract

  #title
  One contract. :br Both ends

  #description
  Describe your app once on the server — events, requests, channels, and streams. `InferClient` mirrors the whole API to the client, flipped to its perspective. Drift becomes a compile error, not a production incident.
  :::

  :::features-feature-channels
  ---
  link: /learn/channels
  link-label: How channels work
  ---
  #headline
  Channels

  #title
  Rooms, scoped :br to their members

  #description
  Declare a room with `base.channel`, gate joins with a `check` you write, and `ctx.channel.broadcast` reaches only its members. Membership lives on the server, so the filtering never leaks to the client — plus presence, online/offline, and dynamic room families.
  :::

  :::features-feature-streaming
  ---
  link: /learn/streaming
  link-label: How streaming works
  ---
  #headline
  Streaming

  #title
  Push data :br as it's made

  #description
  Declare a stream with `base.stream` and an `async *run` generator — every `yield` is one typed chunk on the wire. The client reads them with a `for await` loop as they arrive. Perfect for LLM tokens, with steerable nudges and durable resume across reconnects.
  :::

  #title

  #headline
  AI Observability

  #title
  Make AI calls :br observable

  #description
  Your AI endpoints are black boxes. You don't know how many tokens each request burns, which tools the model called, or how fast the stream was. Wrap your model with one line and every call is captured into the wide event. Cost estimation, tool execution timing, streaming performance, cache hits, reasoning tokens, and multi-step agent breakdowns.
