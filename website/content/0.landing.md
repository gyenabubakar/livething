#headline
The Contract

#title
One contract. :br Both ends

#description
One source of truth for both sides of the wire. Describe your app once on the server, and the client is typed from it automatically — every event, request, channel, and stream, flipped to the client's point of view. Drift between them becomes a red squiggle in your editor, not a 2 a.m. incident.
:::

#headline
Channels

#title
Rooms, scoped :br to their members

#description
Rooms with membership that actually means something. You decide who gets in, and a broadcast reaches exactly the people inside — the rules live on your server, never shipped to the client to bypass. Presence, online/offline, and dynamic room families come in the box.
:::

#headline
Streaming

#title
Push data :br as it's made

#description
Send data to the client the moment it exists — token by token, event by event, each chunk typed end to end. Built for LLM output and live feeds, with nudges to steer a run while it's still going and durable resume that picks up right where a disconnect left off.
:::

#headline
Requests

#title
Ask, and :br await the answer

#description
Real request/response, over a WebSocket. Ask the server a question and `await` a typed reply — no event wiring, no correlation IDs to track by hand. Fire as many as you like on one connection and each answer comes back to the caller that asked, even out of order. And it works both ways: the server can ask the client too.
:::

#headline
Reliability

#title
Disconnected? :br Nothing gets lost

#description
Real networks disconnect you — a sleeping laptop, a flaky tower, a deploy mid-session. LiveThing keeps the connection healthy and your data consistent through all of it: spot a dead socket, reconnect with backoff, hold the sends you made while offline, and replay what you missed without doubles. Stack only the pieces you need.
:::

#headline
Opt-in & tiny

#title
Small :br by default

#description
Small to start, and it stays that way. LiveThing is a thin core you build on, not a runtime you adopt — a typed socket weighs about `4.42 KB` gzipped, and every feature is its own side-effect-free module. Add only the primitives this app needs and leave the rest behind, where their code never ships. Turn everything on and you're still around `18 KB`.
:::

#headline
The Engine

#title
Runtime- and :br transport-agnostic

#description
The engine is LiveThing itself — it holds your contract, routes every frame to the right handler, and runs your channels, requests, streams, and presence. It never opens a socket. An adapter hands it a frame, text or binary, and takes one back. Whether that frame came over a WebSocket, server-sent events, or long polling, on Cloudflare, Node, Bun, or Deno, the engine never finds out.
:::
